The Philosophy

Scaling at the intersection of Design and Engineering.

I’m a systems-thinker with the heart of a tinkerer. I approach a global CCMS migration with the same meticulous curiosity I bring to rebuilding a PC or exploring an unconventional engine. Over the last 9 years, I’ve learned that technical information isn't just "help" content; it’s a core component of the user journey that requires its own scalable infrastructure.

I focus on building "architectures of participation"; ecosystems where engineering, product, and design work in total alignment. Whether it's preparing knowledge ecosystems for LLM consumption (RAG) or reengineering legacy workflows to increase team velocity, I build for the future of retrieval, longevity, and speed.

Architecture

Architecting an API-Driven Content Engine

NETGEAR

The Problem: Documentation was siloed in legacy formats, creating a massive bottleneck for modern AI-assisted discovery tools.

The System: Launched a "Content-as-a-Service" (CaaS) model, migrating 20,000+ topics into a SaaS CCMS (Heretto) to create a single source of truth.

The Result: Established an API-driven infrastructure that is RAG-ready for AI chatbots and automated support agents, significantly increasing content accessibility across the platform.

Efficiency

Slashing Time-to-Market by 95%

NETGEAR

The Problem: Legacy publication cycles carried excessive cross-functional overhead, taking up to three weeks to ship critical updates.
